
# Lost Treasure Adventure

## Game Story

The player arrives on an island to find a lost treasure.
There are three places to explore: a forest, a cave,
and a beach.

The player makes choices to decide what happens next.

## Path 1: Forest

The player enters a forest.

Decision 1:
- Follow the river.
- Follow the animal tracks.

Decision 2:
- Cross the wooden bridge.
- Take the longer path.

Ending:
The player finds a hidden treasure near an old cabin.

## Path 2: Cave

The player enters a dark cave.

Decision 1:
- Go into the left tunnel.
- Go into the right tunnel.

Decision 2:
- Open the wooden door.
- Follow the sound of water.

Ending:
The player discovers a secret treasure room.

## Path 3: Beach

The player walks along the beach.

Decision 1:
- Search near the rocks.
- Explore the old boat.

Decision 2:
- Dig under the palm tree.
- Follow the footprints.

Ending:
The player finds a buried treasure chest.

## Game Features

- The player chooses a path using buttons.
- Each path has two decisions.
- The story changes based on the player's choices.
- The game shows an ending.
- The player can restart the game.
